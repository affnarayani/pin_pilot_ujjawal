Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains clearly what we collect, why, and how it is used. We do not sell your data. Ever.

1. Who We Are

Mind To Better ("we," "us," or "our") operates this website and sells digital ebooks. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle your personal information when you visit our website or make a purchase.

2. Information We Collect

When you purchase an ebook, we collect the following information through our payment processor (Gumroad):

  • Your email address (used to deliver your purchase)
  • Your name (if provided at checkout)
  • Payment information (processed and stored securely by Gumroad — we never see or store your card details)

When you visit our website, we may also collect basic technical data including your browser type, device, and general geographic location (country/region level only) through analytics tools. This data is anonymous and aggregated.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Deliver your purchased ebook and any associated downloadable materials
  • Send you transactional emails (order confirmation, download links)
  • Respond to any support requests you initiate
  • Improve our website and products using anonymous analytics data

We do not use your email for unsolicited marketing unless you explicitly opt in. We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information to any third parties.

4. Payment Processing

All purchases are processed through Gumroad, a third-party payment platform. We do not store any payment card details on our servers. Gumroad's privacy policy governs how your payment information is handled during checkout. We encourage you to review Gumroad's privacy policy at gumroad.com.

5. Cookies

This website uses minimal cookies to ensure basic functionality and to collect anonymous usage analytics. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking cookies. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect certain website features.

6. Data Retention

We retain your purchase records (email address and transaction details) for as long as necessary to fulfill your purchase, handle refund requests within our guarantee period, and comply with any applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for any data processing where consent was given

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Our website uses HTTPS encryption. All payment data is handled exclusively by Gumroad's PCI-compliant infrastructure.

9. Children's Privacy

Our products and website are not directed at individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Questions or Requests?
